## Right view

This photo best shows off the bed, so my idea for it was in my sleep I kick things off the end of my bed all the time, I did this one time and broke my phone.... so I tried to think of a way to fix it and why not put a "slide" at the end of your bed. Also I just thought it would look cool.

## Top view
<img width="237" height="237" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3af48430-10a7-4a05-acf5-a31ab63bfaff" />

This photo best shows off the rug + chairs, both of the chairs are based off of Herman miller. The green/beige chair is loosely based off of their Eames lounge chairs, I've always liked concept of desks/chairs that basically sit on the floor, so I centered my designs around that. The yellow chair is based off of their coconut chair. You can sit in it normal and rock back and forth or flip it around and put it in a corner to lean against. The rug is *very* loosely based off of the aphex twin logo.
